lemma exists_two_k4_faces_cover_bad_edges

lemma covered_boundary_k4_edge_forces_shared_face_hit

    p ∈ sharedFaces M e ∨ q ∈ sharedFaces M e
```

Proof route: `g₃,g₄ ∈ sharedFaces`, hence `g₃,g₄ ∈ σ` by `hU`. Let `a = g₃ ∩ g₄`. Use `tetFaces_pair_inter_card_eq_two` to get `a.card = 2`, then `a ∈ edgesOf σ`. From `hσ.closed a`, the σ-filter of faces containing `a` has card `2`; since `{g₃,g₄}` are two distinct such faces, any covered `p` or `q` in σ containing `a` equals `g₃` or `g₄`.

Then `exists_two_k4_faces_hit_all_bad_flips` is just:
1. get `p q` from `exists_two_k4_faces_cover_bad_edges`;
2. for arbitrary bad `e g₃ g₄`, apply `covered_boundary_k4_edge_forces_shared_face_hit`.

Blocker is only `bad_flip_edge_has_boundary_k4_face`.
