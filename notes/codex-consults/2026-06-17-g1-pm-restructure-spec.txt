2. THE PLAN — carry IsPseudomanifold (NOT a new "Good" disjunction)
=====================================================================
Earlier I thought the IH had to be a disjunction
   Good := IsStickerball  ∨  "two stickerballs joined along an edge"
to survive the case where the auxiliary removed tet `u` has a present flip edge
(so removeTet M u is two pieces, not one sphere-filling). I now believe `Good`
is AVOIDABLE. The two pieces are each a SMALLER single-sphere taut filling, so
the strong-induction IH applies to EACH piece directly; their PM-ness is then
glued by a union lemma. So the carried invariant is simply

   IsPseudomanifold M.support       (alongside the existing FreelyShellable)

and the "two pieces" only ever appears transiently inside one helper lemma.

--- LEMMA A (union-PM).  Pure combinatorics.
If τ = τ₁ ∪ τ₂, IsPseudomanifold τ₁, IsPseudomanifold τ₂, and every tet of τ is
⊆A or ⊆B with (A ∩ B).card ≤ 2 and τ₁ = τ.filter(⊆A), τ₂ = τ.filter(⊆B), then
IsPseudomanifold τ. Proof: a triangle f (card 3) cannot satisfy f ⊆ A∩B
(card ≤2), so every tet containing f lies on a single side ⟹
faceCount τ f = faceCount τᵢ f ≤ 2.

--- LEMMA B (removeTet_isPseudomanifold).  The key helper, parameterized by IH.
Hyp: M taut filling of sphere σ (Unit/closed/taut/simplicial/pure), u EligibleTet,
and IH = "every strictly smaller single-sphere taut filling M' has
IsPseudomanifold M'.support". Conclusion: IsPseudomanifold (removeTet M u).support.
Proof: let {f₃,f₄}=exposedFaces M u, case on FlipEdgePresent σ f₃ f₄.
  • absent (case 1): removeTet M u is itself a smaller single-sphere taut filling
    of flipBoundary σ M u (existing: isSphere2_flipBoundary_of_eligible,
    unitOn_flipBoundary_of_eligible, isTaut_removeTet, simplicialChain_removeTet,
    nrm_removeTet_add_one_of_simplicial). IH ⟹ PM.
  • present (case 2): the case-2 package (flipEdgePresent_side_data, REAL & total
    once H2 is filled — but it does NOT itself need PM to BUILD the package;
    only the bridge field needs H2) splits removeTet M u into M₁ᵘ,M₂ᵘ, each a
    smaller single-sphere taut filling (package fields sphereᵢ,unitᵢ,closedᵢ,
    tautᵢ,simpᵢ,smallerᵢ), with (removeTet M u).support = M₁ᵘ.support ∪ M₂ᵘ.support
    (coverage) and side predicates ⊆A,⊆B, A∩B = f₃∩f₄ card 2. IH ⟹ PM(M₁ᵘ),
    PM(M₂ᵘ); LEMMA A ⟹ PM(removeTet M u).
  CONCERN to check: in case 2 the package is built via flipEdgePresent_side_data
  which depends (through the bridge) on H2. Does Lemma B then have a circular
  dependency on H2? I think NOT, because the SPLIT DATA (sides, spheres,
  smaller, coverage) is produced by flipEdgePresent_side_sets/_algebra, which are
  independent of the bridge (the bridge is a separate field). If needed, factor a
  bridge-free "split package" so Lemma B never touches H2. Please confirm this
  factoring is sound.

--- LEMMA C (triangle rule-out).  Shared faces of an eligible tet vanish on removal.
For e EligibleTet and r ∈ sharedFaces M e: faceCount (removeTet M e).support r = 0.
Proof: suppose r ⊆ w, w ∈ (removeTet M e).support (so w ∈ M.support, w ≠ e).
Take the disjoint eligible u (other half of aleph_disjoint_eligible_pair, so
Disjoint(sharedFaces M e)(sharedFaces M u), u ≠ e). Then r ∈ sharedFaces M e ⟹
r ∉ sharedFaces M u ⟹ (r ∈ (bdry M).support = σ) r ∉ tetFaces u ⟹ r ⊄ u ⟹
u ≠ w (r⊆w) and u ≠ e (r⊆e). So e,w ∈ (removeTet M u).support ⟹
faceCount (removeTet M u).support r ≥ 2. Also r ∈ σ\sharedFaces M u ⊆
flipBoundary σ M u, and UnitOn(bdry(removeTet M u))(flipBoundary σ M u) ⟹
bdry(removeTet M u) r = ±1 ⟹ (faceCount_ne_two_of_boundary) faceCount ≠ 2 ⟹ ≥3 ⟹
(LEMMA B) ¬PM(removeTet M u) — contradiction. So no such w ⟹ faceCount = 0.
