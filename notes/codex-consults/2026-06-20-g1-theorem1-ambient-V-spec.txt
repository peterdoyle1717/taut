The only genuine design question: do we ALSO want Option B — an explicit AMBIENT-ENLARGEMENT INVARIANCE

is mathematically the content of Peter's motivation, but NO current consumer needs it (Cor 1 uses

bdry/nrm/support/Zvol/IsTaut compatibility), not a cleanup of existing code.

  compatible OR a fresh `sgn` argument, since `sgn` depends on the order — flag this as a subtlety: the
