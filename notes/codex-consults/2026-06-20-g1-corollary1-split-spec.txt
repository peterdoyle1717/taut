Last conjunct (filter+filter-neg = M): pure Finsupp, easy.
Clearing: need ONE q clearing M, X, Y together (exists_nat_smul_integral gives q per chain). q•X, q•Y
must be integral (q clears M's denominators, NOT X's, unless q is common). So need a COMMON-clearing lemma.
Then K := toInt(q•M), with bdry K casting to q•(X+Y); qX_int := toInt(q•X), qY_int := toInt(q•Y); by
intToQChain-injectivity, bdry K = qX_int + qY_int, bdry qX_int = 0, qX_int.support = X.support (q>0) so the
support-size hyps transfer. Apply IsTaut.splits_full to K with qX_int, qY_int. Then DIVIDE BACK:
M.filter(·⊆A) = (q:ℚ)⁻¹ • intToQChain (K.filter(·⊆A)) (filter commutes with intToQChain/scale). Qbdry of it
= X (via Qbdry_intToQChain + scale-cancel). IsQTaut of it = the REVERSE transfer.
