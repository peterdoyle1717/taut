PAPER statement + proof (taut.tex)
=====================================================================
Corollary 1 (`\label{cor1}`, taut.tex:681-686): "Qvol adds under almost disjoint union, and for n ≥ 2
taut ℚ-fillings split."
- Qvol = the ℚ-analogue of Zvol (least L¹-norm of a ℚ-chain filling X). Paper: Qvol = Rvol (LP duality);
  the relevant fact here is just the ℚ least-L¹ filling volume.
- The clearing-denominators proof (taut.tex:240-244, the route the paper endorses for reuse):
  "Multiplying a taut ℚ-filling M of X by a common denominator q yields a taut ℤ-filling qM of qX:
  Split the ℤ-filling qM and divide by q to get a splitting of the ℚ-filling M."
  (Alternative route, taut.tex:675: redo the whole Theorem-1 proof over ℚ with "fractional multisets" —
  much more work; the clearing route REUSES the CHECKED integer Theorem 1.)

=====================================================================
LIKELY route (clearing denominators — REUSES integer Theorem 1)
=====================================================================
1. ℚ-filling M of X (a QChain). q := a common denominator of M's coefficients (q : ℕ, q > 0), so qM is an
   INTEGER chain (a `Chain V`). bdry(qM) = q·bdry(M) = q·X (also integral).
2. qM is a taut ℤ-filling of qX (tautness transfers: Qvol-optimality of M ⇒ Zvol-optimality of qM —
   THIS is the delicate step, see pitfalls).
3. Apply IsTaut.splits_full / Zvol_add_of_almost_disjoint_full to qM (qX, qY are integral, support sized
   n+1, A∩B card ≤ n+1 unchanged by scaling).
4. Divide the ℤ-splitting by q to get the ℚ-splitting of M; additivity follows similarly.

=====================================================================
PITFALLS (flag which the route must handle)
=====================================================================
- Support changes under clearing only if zero coeffs mishandled (q > 0 ⇒ supports of M and qM coincide).
- Tautness/minimality over ℚ may NOT transfer to ℤ automatically: need Qvol(qX) = q·Qvol(X) and
  Zvol(qX) = Qvol(qX) (the integral cycle qX has equal ℤ- and ℚ-filling volume? — Qvol ≤ Zvol always; the
  reverse on an integral class is the LP-integrality / the crux). What is the minimal lemma chain here?
- Boundary scaling must be tracked exactly: bdry is ℤ-linear; need a ℚ-linear Qbdry and Qbdry(qM as QChain)
  vs bdry(qM as Chain) compatibility (a coe ℤ→ℚ commuting square).
- Primitive/integral normalization: does the route need M's coeffs to have a common denominator (clear
  via lcm of denominators)? Mathlib has `Rat.den`, `Finset.lcm`; is `qM` integral provable cleanly?
- New rational-chain layer: QChain, Qbdry (ℚ-linear), Qnrm (ℚ or ℚ≥0 L¹), Qvol (sInf over ℚ — note ℚ is
  not well-ordered, so Qvol needs `Real`/`csInf` or a different formulation; HOW to define Qvol so it has
  the needed `Qvol_le` / optimality API? This is a real design question.)
