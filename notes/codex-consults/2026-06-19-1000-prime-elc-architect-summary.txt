Verdict: the `prime_edgeLinkConnected` route is correct only for the `¬ FlipEdgePresent` eligible-removal case. As stated, the companion

`removeTet_edgeLinkConnected : EdgeLinkConnected (removeTet M e).support`

is not valid in the flip-present split case: the remainder is two fillings meeting only along the flip edge, and that edge-link can be disconnected until `e` is reinserted.

Exact recommended patch shape:

1. Do not prove a global `removeTet_edgeLinkConnected`.
2. Add `removeTet_edgeLinkConnected_noFlip`, using IH exactly like `removeTet_isPseudomanifold` case 1.
3. Add an orientation lemma for the survivor gap:
   `eligible_pair_oriented_opp_avoidance`
   returning either
   `¬ (e \ (f₃ ∩ f₄)) ⊆ u`
   or, after swapping,
   `¬ (u \ (g₃ ∩ g₄)) ⊆ e`.
   This uses `exists_two_faces hσ.toClosedSurface`, not a strengthened M25b.
4. Prove `prime_edgeLinkConnected_case1` with `edgeLinkConnected_insert`.
5. Hard-stop before full `taut_edgeLinkConnected` unless you also add a separate flip-present bridge lemma:
   `prime_edgeLinkConnected_case2` or `edgeLinkConnected_insert_flipBridge`.

- avoid modifying `Theorem2Aleph.lean` for M25b unless the orientation lemma fails
