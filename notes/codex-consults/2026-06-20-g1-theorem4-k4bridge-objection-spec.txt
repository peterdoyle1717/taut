COUNTEREXAMPLE (s = empty K4 {A,B,C,D}; bad edge AB)
=====================================================================
Take an eligible tet e = {A,B,P,Q} with P,Q ∉ s, whose two SHARED (boundary) faces are ABP, ABQ. Then
flip edge = ABP ∩ ABQ = AB ∈ s.powersetCard 2 (a K4 edge) — so e is "bad" by your definition.
- ABP, ABQ are boundary (∈ σ); each is in exactly ONE tet (e), by faceCount_eq_one_of_boundary
  (Stickerball.lean:58). Since edgeDeg σ AB = 2 (hσ.closed), {ABP, ABQ} ARE the only two boundary faces
  through AB.
- The K4 faces through AB are ABC, ABD. These are simplices (emptyK4_face_simplex_of_no_emptyK3) but
  NOT necessarily boundary. AB is a boundary edge; its boundary-edge link is a PATH from P to Q, and
  C, D can be INTERIOR vertices along that path (faces ABC, ABD interior). Nothing forces ABC or ABD ∈ σ.
- Then NO boundary K4 face contains AB: the only boundary faces through AB are ABP, ABQ (P,Q ∉ s, so not
  in s.powersetCard 3). Lemma 3's conclusion FAILS.
Key facts (all in repo): faceCount_eq_one_of_boundary (boundary face ⇒ in 1 tet); edgeDeg σ AB = 2
(boundary FACES through AB); but NO lemma bounds TETS-through-an-edge — an edge sits in its whole
edge-link fan, which can be long. So e's shared faces being unique to e does NOT make AB unique to e;
ABC, ABD can live in other (interior) tets of AB's fan.
