THE MATHEMATICAL ARGUMENT (user-supplied correction; this is the intended proof — do not redesign it,
design its Lean implementation).

(A) PURE COMBINATORIAL "hens-and-baskets" lemma.
  - B = finite set of baskets, |B| = n. L = finite list of hens. Each hen h has A(h) ⊆ B with |A(h)| ≤ 2.
  - Coverage: B ⊆ ⋃_{h ∈ L} A(h). Let m = L.length.
  - If m ≤ n − k, then ≥ k hens are "twofers": processing hens in list order, ≥ k hens lay eggs in two
    baskets that were previously empty. For such h, new(h) = A(h) \ (baskets covered by earlier hens)
    are pairwise-disjoint 2-element subsets of B.
  - Proof: new(h) pairwise disjoint, union = B. a_h = |new(h)| ≤ 2, Σ a_h = n. r = #{h : a_h = 2}.
    n = Σ a_h ≤ 2r + 1·(m−r) = m + r, so r ≥ n − m ≥ k.

(B) INSTANTIATION.
  - B = the boundary triangles of σ that must be hit.
  - Hens = SUPPORT tetrahedra of M, counted ONCE each (NOT with coefficient multiplicity).
  - A(u) = boundary triangles of σ that are actual FACES of the support tetrahedron u AND have the
    required proper orientation "at the support-face level."
  - |A(u)| ≤ 2 is the local no-degree-3 fact (a tet shares ≤ 2 faces with σ).
  - Coverage B ⊆ ⋃ A(u) is the ONLY geometric/chain input: every boundary triangle is hit by ≥ 1
    properly-oriented support face. This is WEAKER than faceCount-one and does NOT say a prescribed
    pair is realized by one tet.
  - n − m ≥ maxdeg comes from the existing degree-volume inequality `Zvol_add_deg_le`
    (support.card + deg v (bdry M) ≤ σ.card).
  - Conclusion: ≥ maxdeg twofer support tetrahedra whose two newly-covered boundary triangles are
    mutually disjoint — the disjoint eligible tets for the deletion argument.

  CRITICAL user clarification: coefficients affect the algebraic boundary contribution but do NOT
  create multiple support faces of one tetrahedron. A tet with coefficient 2 still has each face ONCE
  as a support face. The coefficient is irrelevant to A(u); A(u) counts actual faces of the support tet.

(C) MINIMAL-COUNTEREXAMPLE DELETION (the bridge proof).
  - Strong induction on nrm M. Suppose M minimal taut filling of a 2-sphere that is NOT simplicial:
    some t has |M t| ≥ 2.
  - Choose a twofer u (from (B)) whose boundary pair is disjoint from t and the local obstruction, so u≠t.
  - Remove u via the existing removal machinery: M − u is a taut filling of a smaller sphere (or splits),
    nrm (M − u) < nrm M.
  - Persistence: coeff (M − u) t = coeff M t ≥ 2 (removing u ≠ t doesn't touch t). So M − u is still
    NON-simplicial — a smaller counterexample. Contradiction. Hence no t has multiplicity ≥ 2.

is false / not the proof. DO NOT count coefficient copies. DO NOT use a vector-sum lemma.
