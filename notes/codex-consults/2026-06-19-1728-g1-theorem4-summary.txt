Definitions on `τ : Finset (Finset V)`:

- `SimplexOf τ s := s = ∅ ∨ ∃ t ∈ τ, s ⊆ t`
- `EdgeOf τ e := e.card = 2 ∧ SimplexOf τ e`
- `CliqueInOneSkeleton τ s := s ⊆ vertsOf τ ∧ ∀ e, e ⊆ s → e.card = 2 → SimplexOf τ e`
- `IsFlagComplex τ := ∀ s, CliqueInOneSkeleton τ s → SimplexOf τ s`

Then define operational taboos as lemmas/predicates, not the public endpoint:

- `HasEmptyK3 τ`
- `HasEmptyK4 τ`
- `HasK5Clique τ`
- `NoTaboo τ := ¬ HasEmptyK3 τ ∧ ¬ HasEmptyK4 τ ∧ ¬ HasK5Clique τ`

Prove `NoTaboo → IsFlagComplex` for nonempty pure 3-complexes. This is the bridge from paper-proof operational form to the theorem statement.

Q2 route:

Use route (b), with `faceCount` only as a support-control tool.

Given a K5 clique `S.card = 5`, and assuming no empty K3/K4:

- no empty K3 gives every triangle of `S` is contained in some tet;
- no empty K4 upgrades every 4-subset of `S` to a tet in `M.support`;
- hence the five 4-subsets of `S` are exactly the tets in `M.support` contained in `S`;
- for each triangle `f ⊆ S`, exactly two of those five tets contain `f`;
- `taut_isPseudomanifold` / `IsPseudomanifold` gives no third tet contains `f`;
- if `f ∈ σ`, then `bdry M f = ±1` by `UnitOn`, contradicting `faceCount_ne_two_of_boundary`;
- therefore all those triangle boundary coefficients are zero;
- so `U := M.filter (fun t => t ⊆ S)` has `bdry U = 0`;
- `U` is a nonzero subchain of taut `M`, so `hT.subChain (subChain_filter _ M)` makes `U` taut;
- since `bdry U = 0`, prove `Zvol 0 = 0` using `Zvol_le rfl` with `0`, hence `nrm U = 0`;
- contradiction with the five K5 tets in `U.support`.

No `IsSphere3` definition is needed. Avoid it.
