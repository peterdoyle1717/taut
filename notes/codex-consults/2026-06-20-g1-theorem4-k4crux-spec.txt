- A bad tet e (flip edge g₃∩g₄ = a K4 edge XY): its 2 shared faces g₃,g₄ both contain XY and are
  BOUNDARY faces (sharedFaces ⊆ (bdry M).support = σ). Since edgeDeg σ XY = 2, g₃,g₄ ARE the 2 boundary
  σ-faces through XY. So if a boundary K4 face passes through XY, it equals g₃ or g₄.
- Flip edges are boundary edges (g₃∩g₄ ⊆ g₃ ∈ σ ⇒ ∈ edgesOf σ), so the interior K4 edge (AD) is never a
  flip edge; bad flips only hit the ≤5 boundary K4 edges.
- Choose p = ABC, q = BCD (the 2 K4 faces NOT containing the interior edge AD). Per-edge: AB→ABC, AC→ABC,
  BC→{ABC,BCD}, BD→BCD, CD→BCD each have p or q as a boundary K4 face through them, so any bad tet's
  shared pair includes p or q. STATEMENT TRUE.
