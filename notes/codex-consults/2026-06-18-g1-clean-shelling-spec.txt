  := abbrev aliases of the weak defs. GlueStep t B B': card4; (tetFaces t ∩ B).card ∈ {1,2};

[Pseudomanifold.lean]
  TriangleBounded3 τ := IsPseudomanifold τ  (∀ triangle f, faceCount τ f ≤ 2).
  vertexLinkGraph τ v : Adj x y := x ≠ y ∧ ∃ t ∈ τ, {v,x,y} ⊆ t.
  vertexLinkVerts τ v := vertsOf (τ.filter (v ∈ ·)) \ {v}.
  VertexLinkConnected τ := ∀ v, ConnOn (vertexLinkGraph τ v) (vertexLinkVerts τ v).
    proved: vertexLinkConnected_singleton; vertexLinkConnected_insert
      (compat ∀ v ∈ t, vertexLinkVerts τ v = ∅ ∨ ((t\{v}) ∩ vertexLinkVerts τ v).Nonempty);
      not_vertexLinkConnected_of_subset; mono / empty / insert helpers.
  Pure3 τ := ∀ t ∈ τ, t.card = 4.
  Normal3 τ := EdgeLinkConnected τ ∧ VertexLinkConnected τ.
  Clean3Complex τ := Pure3 τ ∧ TriangleBounded3 τ ∧ Normal3 τ.
  clean3Complex_singleton; clean3Complex_insert (combiner; hyps
    hpmc: ∀ triangle f ⊆ t, faceCount τ f ≤ 1; helc; hvlc).

[CleanShelling.lean]

t = {a,b,c,d}. In M: sharedFaces (on bdry M) = {s₁=abc, s₂=abd};
exposedFaces (interior of M) = {r₁=acd, r₂=bcd}.
Remove t: bdry(M−t) = (bdry M \ {s₁,s₂}) ∪ {r₁,r₂}.
Re-glue t onto M−t: B = bdry(M−t), so tetFaces t ∩ B = {r₁,r₂} (the SHARED faces in the
re-glue are the EXPOSED-in-M faces). r₁ ∩ r₂ = cd (the flip edge). Every vertex of t is in
r₁ ∪ r₂; every edge of t is ⊆ r₁ or ⊆ r₂ EXCEPT ab = s₁ ∩ s₂.

Q1 SOUND + COMPLETE + SATISFIABLE. With shared faces {r₁,r₂}:
  (a) does CleanGlueStep.clean rule out all four rogues — doubled tet (f=t), rogue old
      triangle (card 3), rogue old edge (card 2), rogue old vertex (card 1)?
  (b) is it SATISFIABLE in the genuinely-clean re-glue: every old face f ⊆ t present in M−t
      lies in r₁ or r₂? The only obstruction is edge ab (⊄ r₁,r₂): .clean(ab) forces ab ∉ M−t.
      Is "ab ∉ M−t" exactly the case-1 (FlipEdgeAbsent / snoc) branch, and "ab ∈ M−t" exactly
      case-2 (split)? Confirm .clean is the correct discriminator, neither too weak nor too strong.

Q2 CHAIN-DEPENDENCE (the one I am unsure about). To prove "CleanShellFrom prefixes are
  Clean3Complex" we must discharge clean3Complex_insert.hpmc: faceCount τ f ≤ 1 for a triangle
  f ⊆ t in τ. .clean gives f = some g ∈ {r₁,r₂} (both card 3 ⇒ equal). Getting faceCount τ g ≤ 1
  appears to need g a boundary face of the UNIT chain (faceCount_eq_one_of_boundary needs
  bdry = ±1) — i.e. NOT purely combinatorial. Is Clean3Complex-preservation therefore a
  CHAIN-context lemma (carry that B = the unit boundary of M−t), or can .clean be strengthened
  to yield faceCount ≤ 1 combinatorially? Same worry for helc / hvlc: do they need the boundary
  2-sphere's manifold structure, or follow from .clean + the shelling alone?

Q3 THREADING. Base τ={t₁}, accumulate insert t τ ⇒ at the k-th glue τ = {t₁,…,t_{k−1}}.
  Correct for the doubled-tet ruleout (.clean with f=t needs t ∈ the accumulated set)? Off-by-one?

Q4 MIGRATION REUSE. The projections are clean → boundary (forgetful). But the induction
  CONSTRUCTS shellings by reassembly (ShellFrom_append/snoc, IsBall.bridge_of_relShelling). To
  conclude FreelyCleanShellable the reassembly must PRODUCE clean shellings, so we presumably
  need CLEAN analogues of the reassembly lemmas (discharging .clean at each graft); the
  projections only let us reuse boundary FACTS, not build clean shellings. Confirm the real
  migration cost is "lift reassembly to the clean level", and check: is .clean composable across
  append/snoc/bridge (does grafting preserve the no-rogue-face condition, given the grafted tets
  meet the accumulated set only in the shared interface)?
