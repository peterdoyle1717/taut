   169	$X$ the associated integral 2-cycle.
   170	A filling of $X$ is an integral 3-chain $M$
   171	with $\del M = X$;
   172	a taut filling is one with minimal $L_1$-norm.
   173	We show that any taut filling 
   174	arises from an extension of $\sigma$ to
   175	a simplicial complex homeomorphic to the 3-ball.
   176	The filling is \emph{clean}: it has no repeated tetrahedron, and its
   177	support complex is a clean simplicial complex.
   178	This support complex is shellable and flag: every clique in its
   179	1-skeleton occurs as a simplex.
   180	The key to the proof is the general
   181	fact that any taut filling of an $n$-cycle
   182	splits under disjoint union, connected sum, and more generally
   183	what we call almost disjoint union,
   184	where summands are supported on sets that overlap in at most $n+1$ vertices.

   885	of the two smaller fillings after a conjoined split.  This would contradict
   886	minimality, since the smaller filling could not be clean.

   907	component.  The disconnected link remains in the one-sphere case or in one
   908	of the split summands in the conjoined case, contradicting minimality.

   922	triangulation of $B^3$, contradicting that $(\sigma,M)$ was bad.
