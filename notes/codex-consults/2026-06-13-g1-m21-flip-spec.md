## What the induction (M26) will need from M21 (design to this interface)
Given a simplicial taut filling `M` (`bdry M = X`, `UnitOn X σ`), an eligible
tet `t`, the induction forms `M' := M - Finsupp.single t (M t)` and needs:
- `bdry M' = X'` where `X'` is the flipped unit chain, with
  `X'.support = (σ \ {s₁, s₂}) ∪ {f₃, f₄}` (`f₃,f₄` the other two faces of `t`);
- `UnitOn X' σ'` for the new sphere `σ'` (case 1) or the conjoined pair (case 2);
- `M'` still simplicial, `nrm M' = nrm M - 1`;
- a hook to decide case 1 vs 2 (is the flipped edge `cd` already an edge of σ?).
