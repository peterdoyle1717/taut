Verdict: `no_rogue_oppEdge_of_not_flipEdgePresent` is not the right next target as stated. `¬ FlipEdgePresent σ f₃ f₄` does not rule out a fresh tet containing `e \ (f₃ ∩ f₄)`. The missing force is full edge-link connectedness before deleting `e`.

Exact recommended patch shape:
- Do not prove the proposed lemma from `hNotFlip`.
- Add a local lemma consuming full edge-link connectedness:
```lean
private lemma oppEdge_empty_of_full_edgeLinkConnected ...
    (hELM : EdgeLinkConnected M.support) :
    edgeLinkVerts (removeTet M e).support (e \ (f₃ ∩ f₄)) = ∅
```
- Then the real required theorem is:
```lean
theorem taut_edgeLinkConnected {σ : Finset (Finset V)} {X M : Chain V}
    (hσ : IsSphere2 σ) (hU : UnitOn X σ) (hXc : bdry X = 0)
    (hMX : bdry M = X) (hT : IsTaut M) (hS : SimplicialChain M)
    (hPure : ∀ t ∈ M.support, t.card = 4) :
    EdgeLinkConnected M.support
```
Hard stop there: this is a new edge-link connectedness theorem for taut fillings.

Files to edit:
- `lean/Taut/Pseudomanifold.lean` or a new local section in `lean/Taut/PrimeStep.lean`: prove/export `taut_edgeLinkConnected`.
- `lean/Taut/Theorem3Clean.lean`: replace the proposed `hNotFlip` route with `oppEdge_empty_of_full_edgeLinkConnected` once `hELM` is available.

Local lemmas needed for the local `hELM` lemma:
- exposed-pair geometry: from `hexp : exposedFaces M e = {f₃,f₄}`, get vertices `z₃,z₄` with `e \ (f₃ ∩ f₄) = {z₃,z₄}`.
- shared-face identification: the two tet faces of `e` containing `{z₃,z₄}` are exactly the shared faces.
- isolation lemma: in `edgeLinkGraph M.support {z₃,z₄}`, any neighbor of either exposed apex from `e` is the other apex; proof uses `faceCount_removeTet_sharedFace_eq_zero`.
- rogue-apex lemma: if `t ∈ (removeTet M e).support` contains `{z₃,z₄}`, then some apex `w` of `t` lies in `edgeLinkVerts M.support {z₃,z₄}` and `w` is not one of the two apexes from `e`.
- no-reachability lemma: the isolated `{apex₃, apex₄}` component cannot reach `w`.

- `IsPseudomanifold` is insufficient. The required missing theorem is exactly `taut_edgeLinkConnected` above.
