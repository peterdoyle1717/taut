   904	$\sigma$, and $\{a,b,c\}$ and $\{a,b,d\}$ are the two boundary faces adjacent
   905	along it.  There can be at most one such isolated-edge component.  If the
   906	link were disconnected, remove an eligible tet away from this exceptional
   907	component.  The disconnected link remains in the one-sphere case or in one
   908	of the split summands in the conjoined case, contradicting minimality.
   909	
   910	\item
   911	No vertex has disconnected link in $K(M)$.
   912	If $\link(\{a\},K(M))$ were disconnected, the only way a single removal
   913	could connect it would be for one component of the link to be a single
   914	$2$-simplex $\{b,c,d\}$ and for the removed tet to be $\{a,b,c,d\}$.  Then
   915	$\{a,b,c\}$, $\{a,b,d\}$, and $\{a,c,d\}$ would all be boundary faces of
   916	$\sigma$, so $a$ would have degree $3$ in $\sigma$, contrary to primality.
   917	Thus a disconnected vertex link would persist under a suitable smaller
   918	removal and again contradict minimality.
   919	\end{enumerate}
   920	
   921	Thus $M$ is simplicial, $K(M)$ is clean, and $K(M)$ is a simplicial
   922	triangulation of $B^3$, contradicting that $(\sigma,M)$ was bad.
   923	\proofend
